Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679), you have the right to:

To exercise any of these rights, email us at grigoriosdimopulos@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA) at www.dpa.gr.
